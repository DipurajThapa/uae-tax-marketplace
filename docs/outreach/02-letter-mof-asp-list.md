**Subject: Request for guidance on referring to the Ministry's list of accredited e-invoicing service providers**

Dear Sir or Madam,

[Company legal name], licensed in [emirate / free zone] under licence number [number], is preparing to launch [Brand]. It is an online directory where UAE businesses can find e-invoicing service providers and tax agents and contact them. The service is not yet public.

Many businesses will be choosing an e-invoicing provider over the coming months. We would like to help them do so with accurate information. Our approach relies on the list of accredited service providers that the Ministry publishes, and we would like to confirm that our approach is acceptable to the Ministry.

**What we intend to do**

1. Providers will appear in the directory only after they register with us. We will not copy, download or republish the Ministry's list.
2. When a provider says it is accredited, our staff will check that statement against the Ministry's published list, one provider at a time, and record the date of the check.
3. A provider's profile will say that its accreditation was checked against the Ministry's list on that date. The profile will link to the Ministry's page so that users can check it themselves.
4. We will check again at regular intervals and remove the indication if a provider no longer appears on the list.
5. We will not suggest that the Ministry approves, recommends or endorses the directory or any provider in it.

**Our request**

We would be grateful if the Ministry could:

(a) confirm whether the list of accredited service providers is available under the Ministry's Open Data Policy and, if so, on what terms; or

(b) grant written consent for the limited use described above; or

(c) point us to any official channel for checking a provider's accreditation that the Ministry would prefer us to use.

We would be happy to provide further details or a demonstration. Please contact [name, title] at [email] or [telephone].
