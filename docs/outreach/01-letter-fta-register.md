**Subject: Request for consent to verify, and to display the verification status of, tax agents listed in the Register of Tax Agents**

Dear Sir or Madam,

[Company legal name] is a company licensed in [emirate / free zone] under licence number [number]. We are preparing to launch [Brand], an online directory that helps businesses in the UAE find a qualified tax agent or e-invoicing service provider and contact them. The service is not yet open to the public.

We are writing before launch because our approach depends on the Register of Tax Agents published on the Authority's website. We want to be sure that the way we intend to use it is acceptable to the Authority.

**What we intend to do**

1. Tax agents will join the directory only by registering themselves. We will not copy, download or republish the Register, in whole or in part.
2. When an agent asks to show their listing status, a member of our staff will look up the agent's Tax Agent Approval Number on the Authority's public Register, one record at a time. We will record the date of the check.
3. If the record matches, the agent's profile will say that the listing was checked against the Authority's Register on that date. The profile will link to the Register so that users can check it themselves.
4. We will check each listing again at regular intervals. We will remove the indication at once if the record no longer appears, or if the agent's status changes.
5. We will never describe the directory, or any agent on it, as approved or endorsed by the Authority. Our pages will say that the directory is independent of the Authority.

**Our request**

We understand from clause [9] of the Authority's website Terms and Conditions that content on the website may not be stored or reproduced for commercial purposes without the Authority's prior written consent. We therefore respectfully request:

(a) the Authority's written consent to the limited use described above, namely storing, for each agent who has registered with us, the approval number, the name as it appears on the Register and the date of our check, and showing that status on the agent's profile; or

(b) if the Authority prefers, guidance on an approved channel for verifying listing status, such as an official verification service or data-sharing arrangement. We would be glad to use such a channel on any terms the Authority sets.

If the Authority considers that the use described in points 1 to 5 needs no consent, a short confirmation to that effect would also be very helpful to us.

We would welcome the opportunity to explain the service in more detail or to show it to the Authority's staff before launch. Please contact [name, title] at [email] or [telephone].

Thank you for considering our request.

Yours faithfully,

[Signature]

[Full name]
