# Provider outreach plan and message drafts

Status: DRAFT. Nothing here is sent until the owner approves it. Revised after `docs/research/legal-due-diligence-2026-10.md` Q9:
- Under the PDPL, direct marketing needs prior consent, and there is no legitimate-interests basis.
- The TDRA policy expects consent, an unsubscribe option and consent records.

So this plan writes only to the generic role addresses that firms publish (for example info@), never to a named person's email, until counsel confirms otherwise.

## 1. Goal

Get the first 30–50 real providers to list themselves before public launch:
- Corporate Tax and VAT firms with FTA-listed agents.
- E-invoicing providers accredited by the Ministry of Finance.
- A mix of emirates, firm sizes and languages.

A second goal is to hold 10 or more pricing interviews (see `docs/research/market-and-pricing-2026-10.md`).

## 2. Ground rules

1. **No bulk use of official registers.** We do not build a mailing list from the FTA Register of Tax Agents or the MoF list, because their terms restrict reuse. We may look up a single firm to confirm a registration it has told us about.
2. **Where contacts come from:**
   - the firm's own website: only its published **generic role address** (info@, enquiries@), never a named person's address;
   - LinkedIn: only after counsel confirms how the PDPL consent rule applies to platform messages; until then, reply only to people who contact us first;
   - introductions from people we know;
   - professional events: ACCA, ICAEW and CIMA Middle East chapters, chamber of commerce and free-zone business events;
   - firms that approach us.
3. **One-to-one messages only.** Each email:
   - is written to one firm and sent from a named person;
   - says who we are, why we are writing and where we found the address;
   - includes a one-click way to opt out;
   - is sent between 07:00 and 21:00 UAE time.

   No purchased lists, no mail-merge blasts, no tracking pixels, and no copying contact details from the FTA or MoF lists.
4. **Easy opt-out.** Anyone who says no, or does not reply after one follow-up, is not contacted again. We keep a do-not-contact list.
5. **What we record:** firm name, source of the contact, date of each message and the response. This shows that each contact was legitimate if anyone asks.
6. **Copy rules:**
   - Never say or suggest that we are approved by the FTA or MoF.
   - Never promise a number of leads or clients.
   - Never use "guaranteed", "best", "you should", or any savings claim.

## 3. Sequence

| Week | Activity | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Owner's own network: warm introductions | 10–15 firms |
| 1–2 | Pricing interviews with willing firms (30 minutes, video or phone) | 10+ |
| 2–4 | Individual emails to firms found on their own websites, by emirate and service | 15–20 per week |
| 2–4 | LinkedIn notes: on hold until counsel confirms (see section 2) | — |
| 3–6 | One or two professional events; collect cards with permission | — |
| Ongoing | Follow up once, after 7 days, then stop | — |

Track the funnel in a simple sheet with these stages: contacted → replied → interview → account created → registration verified → profile published.

## 4. Message drafts

### 4a. First email (cold, one firm)

**Subject:** Listing [Firm name] on a new UAE tax agent directory

Dear [Firm name] team,

I am [your name], founder of Taxdar, a directory for UAE businesses looking for tax agents and e-invoicing providers. We have not launched yet. I am contacting a small number of firms that we would like to include from the start, and [Firm name] is one of them because of [specific reason: your corporate tax work for family businesses in Sharjah / your Arabic and English service / your published e-invoicing guidance].

How it works, briefly:
- businesses answer a few questions about what they need;
- they see the firms that fit, with the reason for each match;
- they contact up to three of them;
- registrations are checked by our staff before a profile shows them as verified;
- paying for a plan does not move a firm up the results.

A basic listing is free. Paid plans are for firms that want more enquiries each month.

Would you be open to a 20-minute call in the next two weeks? I would value your view on how firms like yours find new clients, and on what a fair price for this kind of service would be.

I found this address on your website, [website]. If this is not of interest, reply "no thanks" or use this link: [opt-out link]. Either way, we will not contact you again.

Kind regards,

[Full name]
[Title], [Company legal name]
[Phone] · [Website]
[Company address and licence number]
