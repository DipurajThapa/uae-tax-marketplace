# Brand name options (replacing "TaxPro Directory UAE")

- Prepared: 2026-10-08. Research by an automated agent using web search only. This is not legal advice or a trademark clearance opinion.
- Backlog item: BIZ-01 (owner decision). Related counsel item: LG-07.

## Summary

1. **Primary pick: Taxdar** (Arabic: تاكسدار), pronounced "TAX-dar" (rhymes with "radar"). It carries the keyword "tax" and reads as a unique coined word. The search found no company, app or site with this name.
2. **Shortlist, ranked:** Taxdar (30/35), TaxDaleel (27), TaxRoster (24), Sadeed (23), Tabeen (23).
3. **TaxDaleel** is the strongest bilingual option, but it carries two risks. The fintech "Daleel" already trades in the UAE, and the FTA and MoF publish official guides called "دليل". An Arabic rendering such as "دليل الضرائب" could therefore read as an official FTA guide.
4. **Rejected for legal-naming or collision reasons:** Wakeel (Al-Wakeel is a divine name), Thiqa (an Abu Dhabi government programme), "UAE…" and "Emirates…" names (restricted in trade names), Fatoorly (echoes ZATCA's FATOORA), TaxMatch (TaxMatch Japan offers the same service), Mizan, Wazin, Daleel, Khibra and Sanad.
5. **Domains and trademarks are UNVERIFIED.** RDAP lookups were refused by the session's proxy (organisation policy) and WebFetch could not resolve rdap.verisign.com. Official trademark and trade-name registries were not searched.
6. **Before buying anything, the owner should** check domains at a registrar, get a trademark-agent search (MoE register, WIPO Global Brand Database), check trade-name availability with DET or MoE, get native Arabic review, and get counsel's view on LG-07 (section 5.2).
7. **Trademark classes:** file in Nice classes 35 and 42, add class 9 if a mobile app is planned, and treat class 45 as optional. A trademark agent should confirm the classes.
8. **Descriptor line:** "Taxdar — UAE tax agent and e-invoicing provider directory". Every surface should also carry the line "Independent. Not affiliated with the FTA or the Ministry of Finance."

## 1. Method and limits

- **Sources.** All findings come from web-search excerpts. Official pages such as investindubai.gov.ae and u.ae could not be opened (DNS failure), which is the same limit recorded in `stage0-regulatory-ledger.md`. Tier labels follow that ledger: T1-snippet means an official page seen only as a search excerpt, and T2 means a secondary source such as a consultancy or registrar.
- **Domains.** Three curl requests went to `rdap.org` and seven to `rdap.verisign.com`. The proxy rejected all of them with `connect_rejected` (organisation policy). WebFetch failed with `ENOTFOUND`. No workaround was attempted, so **every domain status below is UNVERIFIED**.
- **Collision searches.** These were web searches, mostly in English plus some in Arabic. No search was run on the official registers: the MoE trademark e-services, the DET trade-name search or the WIPO Global Brand Database. A clean web search does not mean a name is clear to use.

## 2. Naming rules that constrain the choice

| Rule | What the sources say | Effect on this brand | Source (tier) |
|---|---|---|---|
| No divine names or attributes | Dubai: a trade name may not contain Allah's name, "God" or His divine attributes | Excludes Wakeel (Al-Wakeel), Haseeb, Khabeer, Rasheed, Hakeem, Adl, Qayyum-like words, and others | Invest in Dubai "request to book a trade name" (T1-snippet); Alkabban, Gulf News (T2) |
| No implied government link | Names implying a government body or connection are rejected at screening | Excludes "FTA", "Federal", "Authority", "Ministry", "Official", "Gov"; also Rasmi ("official"), Sijil/Tasjeel ("register"), Mo'tamad ("accredited"), Thiqa (an Abu Dhabi government programme), Darb (Abu Dhabi's toll system), and anything close to EmaraTax (the FTA platform) or FATOORA (ZATCA) | Dubai South BH, Sarmat, CMA list (T2) |
| Place names restricted | "UAE", "Emirates", emirate and city names and airport codes need special approval; nationality adjectives are allowed | Descriptive names such as "UAE Tax Agents" are poor trade names. A place can still go in a descriptor or a domain, but not in the legal name | IFZA guide, Flying Colour, RAKEZ (T2) |
| Scope words | A name may not start with "International", "Global", "Middle East" and similar words | Avoid "Global Tax…" | IFZA, DMCC guidelines (T2) |
| Religion, sects, offence | Religious, sectarian, political or offensive terms are refused | Avoid Quranic-only terms such as Bayyina and Mizan, and words with negative senses such as Qareen (a jinn companion) | Dubai South BH, Gulf News (T2) |
| Similarity | A name must differ from existing trade names, known brands and government projects, and DET may cancel a similar name | This is why collision checks matter | Invest in Dubai (T1-snippet); Al Tamimi (T2) |
| Transliteration | The Arabic form is a transliteration, not a translation | The Arabic trade name for Taxdar would be تاكسدار, not دار الضرائب | Invest in Dubai (T1-snippet) |
| Activity fit | The name should fit the licensed activity, and some regulated words need the regulator's approval | **Open:** a legal name containing "Tax" for a platform or e-commerce licence may draw questions. The legal entity name can differ from the brand (counsel item LG-07) | Sarmat, Virtuzone (T2) |

The brand, the trademark and the legal trade name are three separate things. The brand can be "Taxdar". The licensed entity could use a name such as "Taxdar Technologies" plus its legal-form suffix, subject to what the licensing authority accepts.

## 4. Shortlist

### 4.1 Taxdar (primary pick)

| Item | Detail |

It names no emirate, so it works across all seven, and nothing in it suggests a government body. |

## 5. Recommendation: Taxdar

Taxdar is the pick because it scores highest on the combination of keyword fit, distinctiveness and low legal and collision risk. TaxDaleel would be the stronger bilingual name, but both of its issues fall under the hard constraints: it shares its distinctive element with a UAE financial marketplace, and in Arabic it reads like a government guide. Keep TaxDaleel as the fallback if Taxdar fails registry clearance or its domains are unavailable.

### 5.2 Owner actions before adopting the name (purchases need owner approval)

1. Check availability at an accredited registrar for taxdar.ae, taxdar.com and تاكسدار.امارات, and for the fallback taxdaleel.ae and taxdaleel.com.
   - The task brief says .ae needs UAE presence. Registrar sources say first-level .ae is open to anyone, and that the trade-licence or trademark requirement applies to .co.ae and other second-level zones. These sources conflict and are not official; check the current aeDA policy on tdra.gov.ae.
2. Have a trademark agent search the UAE MoE trademark register (English and Arabic, classes 35, 42 and 9) and the WIPO Global Brand Database.
3. Check trade-name availability with DET (or the chosen free zone) for a legal name built on "Taxdar".
4. Ask a native Arabic speaker to review تاكسدار and the Arabic descriptor.
5. Get counsel's view on LG-07.
